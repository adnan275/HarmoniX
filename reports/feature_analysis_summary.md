## Reading these results

- Correlations and importances are associations in this dataset, not causal effects. A feature can rank highly because it co-varies with something else listeners respond to.
- Valence and arousal are correlated in PMEmo, so the partial correlation is the better guide to whether a feature relates to valence beyond general energy/intensity.
- Impurity (MDI) importance is biased towards continuous, high-variance features; permutation importance and SHAP are the more reliable rankings.
- Strongly correlated features (see the heatmaps) share credit: shuffling one of them barely hurts the model when another carries the same information, so a low permutation score does not mean the feature is unrelated to valence.
- HNR and sensory dissonance are simplified signal-based estimates, not validated psychoacoustic measurements.
- Valence is a proxy for pleasantness-related perception, not a universal measure of musical pleasantness.
